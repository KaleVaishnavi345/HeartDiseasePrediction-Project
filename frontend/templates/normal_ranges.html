Normal Range Guide for Input Fields

Use this guide before filling the heart disease prediction form.

These ranges are for awareness only and can vary with age, gender, and medical conditions. Clinical advice from a doctor always has higher priority than this guide.
Input Normal / Preferred Range Meaning for Patients
Age 18 to 100 years (model input range) Risk generally increases with higher age.
Resting Blood Pressure (`trestbps`) Around 90 to 120 mmHg Consistently high BP can increase heart disease risk.
Cholesterol (`chol`) Below 200 mg/dL preferred Higher cholesterol can lead to plaque buildup in arteries.
Fasting Blood Sugar (`fbs`) Normal: `No` (<= 120 mg/dL) Higher fasting sugar is linked with metabolic and heart risk.
Resting ECG (`restecg`) Normal: value `0` Abnormal ECG values may indicate electrical changes in the heart.
Maximum Heart Rate (`thalach`) Often healthy when not too low for age Very low exercise heart-rate response can suggest reduced fitness/cardiac function.
Exercise Induced Angina (`exang`) Preferred: `No` Chest pain during exercise may indicate ischemic stress.
ST Depression (`oldpeak`) Closer to 0 is better Higher ST depression can indicate stress-related heart strain.
ST Slope (`slope`) Preferred: Upsloping (`0`) Flat/downsloping patterns are often associated with higher risk findings.
Major Vessels (`ca`) 0 is ideal More affected vessels can indicate more severe coronary involvement.
Thalassemia (`thal`) Preferred: Normal (`1`) Defect results can be associated with perfusion abnormalities.
Chest Pain Type (`cp`) No single normal value Asymptomatic or angina patterns may carry different risk implications.
Sex (`sex`) No normal/abnormal classification This is a demographic feature used by the prediction model.
